Get to know us

Our Story

In light of the rapid development of the people and the world, and the quantum leap of the global economic market, we decided to join the development and create a logistics revolution that reflects continuous growth and development — by creating a company dedicated to providing innovative, professional, modern solutions to become the distinctive mark in warehousing and logistics services.

Our Mission

Providing logistical support for trading companies to maintain their sustainability, facilitate the flow of goods between suppliers and markets to final consumers, and prevent the supply chain from stopping or being obstructed.

Our Vision

To be an ideal, safe and professional logistics environment with the highest level of equipped transport and distribution fleets, to serve customers and achieve their goals — a leader in its field and a pioneer in the region.

Our Values

Our Values

Our values at ING are honesty and trust, sincerity, integrity and partnership in pursuit of productivity.

  • Honesty and trust Honesty in words and deeds, and trust in everything we do with our clients and partners.
  • Sincerity in work Sincerity and dedication in delivering a service worthy of our clients' trust.
  • Integrity Transparency and integrity in every dealing, and commitment to professional standards.
  • Partnership and productivity One-team spirit and participation in hard work to achieve productivity.
